Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GTA6 Grind (“we”, “us”) is an unofficial fan project that shares short GTA 6 videos on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. This policy explains what information we handle. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. This website

This website is a simple information page. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no comments. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation of the site.

2. Information you send us

If you email us (for example to ask for a clip to be removed), we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and handle your request, and we do not sell or share it.

3. How we use TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

We use a private tool that connects to our own TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts through those platforms' official APIs. Its only purpose is to publish our own videos and captions to our own accounts.

  • It does not collect information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok, Instagram or Facebook users.
  • It does not read other people's accounts, messages or private content.
  • The access tokens it uses are stored privately on our own computer. We do not share, sell or publish them.
  • Authorization can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services).

When you watch, like, comment on or share our videos on those platforms, the platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies: TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. We do not control those.

4. Third-party content

Our videos feature clips made by other creators, who are credited in our captions. Those clips can show the creators and the people who appear in their streams. If you appear in a clip and want it removed, contact us and we will act promptly.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that run this website, or when the law requires it.

6. Retention and security

We keep emails only as long as needed to deal with your request. We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, but no system is completely secure.

7. Children

Our content and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to object to how we use it. Contact us below and we will respond.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
